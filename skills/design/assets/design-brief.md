## Scope and evidence

Primary user/job, requested outcome, non-goals, existing system/reference sources,
assumptions and unresolved decisions that materially affect implementation.

## Direction

One specific composition and visual language, with its reason. State what stays
from the existing product, what changes and what would violate the direction.

## Journey and content

Entry → decision → primary action → outcome → recovery. Identify actual copy/data,
local demonstration content and missing assets or integrations.

## Implementation contract

Layout at narrow/wide widths; token/component ownership; typography and imagery;
necessary empty/loading/error/success/disabled states; focus/keyboard behavior;
motion and reduced-motion treatment. Give concrete rules rather than adjectives.
